The Pakistani man is incredulously petty.

He will refuse to put in the effort. He will lie. He will cheat. He is
deceitful, and he is lazy. Despite all of the above, he cares naught, for
personal benefit, excellence, or reward, whence observing - and acutely does he
observe, in such matters - that others may be benefitting more than him.

He shan't procure forth an ounce of logic or attempt to intuit the reason(s)
behind the 'other' receiving a more magnanimous reward than he. He shan't be
content with his own reward, nay, he will whine, complain, question,
interrogate, inquire, and be consumed by that dark, horrid thought loitering in
the back of his skull.

Yours truly has witnessed nary a socio-economic stratum of Lahori society where
the aforementioned behaviour has yet to transpire.

Be it at the workplace, when an employee learns that another got a larger
increment (whilst conveniently failing to notice said party putting in overtime
hours), or in mine humble capacity as a teaching assistant, when a student cared
naught for his own (significantly-inflated) grade, but only that he obtained
less than another whom he deemed less capable.

Indubitably, such behaviour is virulently ubiquitous amongst all manner of
Pakistani merchants too. From men commanding vast swathes of wealth arguing over
paltry sums out of fear of compensating the other party an iota too much, down
to the humble Pathan, selling fries at his stall, seething at a quick-witted
juvenile for reselling his boxes of fries at a Rs. 50 premium, failing to
observe that not only is he selling more units via the youth, but also reaching
new whereabouts previously unbeknownst.

But alas, such is the plight of the Islamic Republic; her coffers doomed to lay
barren till the day the Pakistani man may acquiesce to a negotation reckoning
not the boon of another, but his own.
